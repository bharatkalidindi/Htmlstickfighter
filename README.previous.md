# HTML Stick Fighter

A browser-based stick fighting game built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

## Original Creator

**Bharat Kalidindi**

Original game concept, design and initial implementation:

https://github.com/bharatkalidindi/Htmlstickfighter

This project is open source and welcomes contributions from the community.

📱 How to Play on Mobile

HTML Stick Fighter can be played on an Android phone using Google Chrome.

Step 1 — Download the Game

Download the HTML game file to your phone.

Open the downloaded ".html" file with Google Chrome.

Step 2 — Enable Desktop Site

After opening the game in Chrome:

1. Tap the ⋮ three-dot menu in Chrome.
2. Enable Desktop site.
3. Set the Chrome page zoom to approximately 50%.

This gives the game enough screen space for the controls and gameplay area.

Step 3 — Enter Fullscreen

Inside the game, press the Fullscreen option.

Once fullscreen is enabled, turn your phone to landscape orientation.

You are now ready to play.

🎮 How to Play

HTML Stick Fighter currently contains 8 levels.

Each level introduces greater difficulty, requiring faster reactions, better movement and more effective use of the available attacks and abilities.

🕹️ Mobile Controls

Left Side — Movement

The left side of the screen contains the movement controls:

◀ Left
Move the fighter to the left.

▶ Right
Move the fighter to the right.

Right Side — Combat & Movement

The right side contains the fighter's action controls:

⚔️ Attack
Attack your opponent.

⬆️ Jump
Jump over attacks and obstacles.

🔄 Flip
Perform a flip movement and use it to avoid attacks or reposition your fighter.

Additional attack/action buttons may be available depending on the level.

---

🔵 Blue Power Dots

During some levels, you will see blue dots on the game screen.

Touch or collect these blue dots to obtain additional powers.

They are not simply decoration—collecting them can give you an advantage during the more difficult parts of the game.

Look for them while fighting and moving through the level.

---

⚔️ Eight Levels

The game contains 8 progressively difficult levels.

As you advance:

- Enemies become more challenging.
- Timing becomes more important.
- You need to combine movement and attacks.
- Jumping and flipping become useful for avoiding attacks.
- Power dots can provide additional advantages in selected levels.
- Later levels require better control of the fighter.

Try to learn the movement and attack combinations in the early levels before progressing to the more difficult stages.

---

💡 Gameplay Tips

Keep Moving

Don't remain in one position for too long. Use left, right, jump and flip to avoid enemy attacks.

Combine Movement and Attacks

Instead of repeatedly pressing the attack button, experiment with attacking while moving, jumping or flipping.

Watch for Blue Dots

When you see a blue power dot, consider collecting it if it is safe to do so. The additional power can become particularly useful as the difficulty increases.

Learn the Enemy

Each level becomes progressively harder. Observe enemy movement and learn when to attack and when to evade.

Master the Controls

The game is designed around a combination of:

Movement + Attack + Jump + Flip + Power Collection

Learning to combine these actions is more important than simply pressing the attack button repeatedly.

---

📱 Recommended Mobile Setup

For the current version of the game, the recommended setup is:

Chrome → Desktop Site → Zoom 50% → Game Fullscreen → Landscape Mode

Once this is configured, the game provides a larger gameplay area while keeping the touch controls accessible on both sides of the screen.
