# HTML Stick Fighter

A browser-based stick fighting game built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, playable on mobile by touch and on desktop with the keyboard.

The fight is drawn in 2D. Kills, the lion's big moves, leisure moments and the final battle are shown in lightweight 3D (three.js).

## 📱 How to Play on Mobile

HTML Stick Fighter can be played on an Android phone using Google Chrome.

### Step 1 — Download the Game

Download the HTML game file to your phone.

Open the downloaded `.html` file with Google Chrome.

> The 3D scenes use three.js, which loads from the internet the first time you tap or press a key. Without an internet connection the game still works fully in 2D. The 3D scenes are simply skipped, and level 9 is played in 2D.

### Step 2 — Enable Desktop Site

After opening the game in Chrome:

1. Tap the ⋮ three-dot menu in Chrome.
2. Enable **Desktop site**.
3. Set the Chrome page zoom to approximately **50%**.

This gives the game enough screen space for the controls and gameplay area.

### Step 3 — Enter Fullscreen

Inside the game, press the **Fullscreen** button.

Once fullscreen is enabled, turn your phone to landscape orientation.

You are now ready to play.

## 🎮 How to Play

HTML Stick Fighter contains **9 levels**.

Each level adds difficulty and requires faster reactions, better movement and smarter use of your attacks and powers.

### 🕹️ Mobile Controls

**Left side — movement**

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| ⇦ Left | Move left |
| ⇨ Right | Move right |

**Right side — combat and movement**

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| ⚔️ Slash | Attack. Tap repeatedly for a 3-hit combo |
| ⬆️ Jump | Jump. Tap again in the air for a double jump |
| 🔄 Flip | Backflip to dodge attacks and reposition |
| Spin / Dash | Spinning attack and a fast dash strike |

More buttons appear on some levels: Ground Pound, Grapple, Grenade, Arrow, God Mode powers, and Thunder / Snakes / Rain in level 9.

**Top buttons**

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| Skip Level | Jump to the next level |
| Leisure | Pause the fight for a leisure break (levels 1–7) |
| Fullscreen | Play in fullscreen |

During a 3D kill-cam or lion cam, **tap anywhere to skip** it.

### ⌨️ Keyboard Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| A / D | Move left / right |
| W | Jump (press again in the air to double jump) |
| F | Slash (combo) / Arrow on the tree in level 6 |
| G | Spin / Grenade / Thunder (level 9) |
| H | Dash / Snakes (level 9) |
| Shift | Flip / Rain (level 9) |
| S | Ground Pound (from level 4) |
| R | Grapple when powered up (level 5) / Restart after game over |
| L | Leisure mode (levels 1–7) |

## 🔵 Blue Power Dots

During some levels, you will see blue dots on the game screen.

Touch or collect these blue dots to get extra powers, such as a glowing blade, bullets, grapple, grenades or arrows depending on the level.

They are not just decoration. Collecting them can give you an advantage in the harder parts of the game, so look for them while fighting and moving through the level.

## ⚔️ The Nine Levels

| Level | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Dual Swords.** Learn slash, spin, dash and flip |
| 2 | **Tougher foes.** Your blade ignites with power |
| 3 | **Gun & Sword.** Powered slashes fire bullets in four directions |
| 4 | **Ground Pound** unlocked. Enemies go flying, and power bullets return |
| 5 | **Helicopters.** Power-up grants the Grapple |
| 6 | **Lions.** Fight lions that roar, stalk, pounce, pin and swipe. Mash buttons to break free when pinned. Climb the tree for arrows |
| 7 | **The path seems clear…** but something is waiting |
| 8 | **God Mode Battle.** Counter the attacks with divine powers |
| 9 | **Final Battle — the Soul Duel.** One on one with the Devil Soul |

As you advance:

- Enemies become more challenging, and tough enemies can block your hits.
- Timing becomes more important.
- You need to combine movement and attacks.
- Jumping and flipping become useful for avoiding attacks.
- Power dots provide extra advantages in selected levels.

Try to learn the movement and attack combinations in the early levels before moving on to the harder stages.

## 👹 Level 9 — The Soul Duel (Final Battle)

After God Mode, the hero faces the **Devil Soul**: a tall, glowing spirit warrior with a soul blade.

- The whole fight is in **3D**, on one plain stone arena with a rune circle, fire braziers and a red moon.
- The camera follows both fighters.
- The hero wears **golden armour**: a crested helmet with a glowing visor, a chest plate, a red cape and golden blades. He deals more damage and takes less in this fight.
- The Devil Soul walks the arena and attacks with charging slashes, fireballs, fire pillars and fire storms. As he weakens, he adds a **jump-and-slam** with a shockwave.

**Your powers** (each has a cooldown bar under the lives counter):

| Power | Button / Key | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| ⚡ Thunder | THN / G | A lightning bolt strikes and stuns the Devil Soul |
| 🐍 Snakes | SNK / H | Three homing snakes bite and poison him |
| 🌧️ Rain | RAIN / Shift | Holy rain heals you, burns him and puts out his fireballs |

Hit him hard enough and he **staggers**. Defeat him to win the game.

## ☕ Leisure Mode (Levels 1–7)

Tap **Leisure** (or press **L**) and the whole fight freezes. The scene dims and a spotlight falls on your hero, who takes a break:

**Levels 1–4 — Style**
- 🚶 **Style walk.** A swagger with one hand in his pocket, swords crossed on his back.
- 🚬 **Cigar break (in 3D).** He lights a cigar, the tip glows, and he blows smoke while the camera circles him.
- 💃 **Dance.** Jumps, kicks, disco points and a spinning jump to a bass beat.

**Levels 5–7 — Rest**
- 😮‍💨 **Catching breath.** Slumped on the ground, tired.
- 🤔 **Deep thought.** Sitting with a thought bubble.
- 🧘 **Meditation.** Cross-legged, floating with a calm glow.
- 🪨 **On the edge (in 3D).** Sitting on the edge of the floor, legs swinging over the drop.

Press **Leisure**, **L**, or any move or attack to get back to the fight.

## 💡 Gameplay Tips

**Keep moving.** Don't stay in one position for too long. Use left, right, jump and flip to avoid enemy attacks.

**Use combos.** Tap Slash in rhythm for a 3-hit combo. The third hit knocks enemies down.

**Watch for the glint.** A flash on an enemy's sword means it is about to strike, so move or flip away.

**Watch for blue dots.** When you see a blue power dot, collect it if it is safe. The extra power is especially useful as the difficulty rises.

**Learn the enemy.** Observe enemy movement and learn when to attack and when to evade. In level 6, watch the lion crouch before it pounces.

**In the Soul Duel,** save Rain for when your health is low or the screen fills with fireballs, and use Thunder to stop his big attacks.

**Master the controls.** The game is built around a combination of:

Movement + Attack + Jump + Flip + Power Collection

Learning to combine these actions matters more than pressing the attack button repeatedly.

## 🛠️ Technical Notes

- Single HTML file. No build step and no install.
- 2D gameplay on an HTML5 canvas at a fixed 60 steps per second, so it plays the same on 60, 90 and 120 Hz screens.
- 3D scenes use **three.js r128**, loaded once from cdnjs on the first tap or key press.
  - Lightweight settings for mobile: low-poly models, simple lighting, no shadows, no antialiasing, pixel ratio capped at 1.5.
- Sound is generated live with the Web Audio API through a mastering chain: bass boost, a cut in the harsh range, a high cut and a compressor.
- Test links: add `#level=6` to the file's URL to start on a level (1–9), and `#three` to load the 3D scenes immediately.
